## Tone notes

- Don't say "kinda" or "basically" or "literally" . hackathon judges punish hedging language.
- Say "mainnet" out loud at least twice. Once in Beat 2, once in Beat 8.
- Say "verifiable" at least twice. Once in Beat 3, once in Beat 17.
- Resist saying "yield." We documented that the yield routing is stubbed in v1. Stick to "exposure budget" and "shield." Honesty wins; judges will quiz you on the README if claims drift.
- Read MetaMask popup contents aloud the first time it appears so listeners understand what they're seeing. Don't narrate every subsequent popup.
